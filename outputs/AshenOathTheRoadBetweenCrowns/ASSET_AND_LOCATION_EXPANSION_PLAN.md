# Asset And Location Expansion Plan

## Rules

Reuse current modular village, forest, cemetery, shrine, ruins, enemy, and prop assets. New assets must be small, legal, CC0/free, and introduced only by the ticket that needs them. Prefer generated materials and recombination over full packs.

## Recommended Build Order

1. Cemetery and chapel.
2. Deeper Wychwood encounter pocket.
3. Old mill.
4. Bandit road and burned farmstead.
5. Castle approach and record hall.
6. White Hart Glade.

Each location is a bounded section with one landmark, one route, one investigation loop, one aftermath state, and a Potato fallback. Do not download a complete environment pack to solve one prop need.
