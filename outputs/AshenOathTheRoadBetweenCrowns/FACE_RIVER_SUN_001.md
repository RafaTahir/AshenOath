# FACE-RIVER-SUN-001

## Implemented

- Bank-changing NPC routes are expanded through bridge entrance, centre, and exit anchors.

- A shared 1K facial atlas provides human age/complexion variation and restrained horror-oriented monster faces.
- The oversized orange sphere and decorative sun rays are replaced by a small warm-white celestial disc and soft halo.
- Cloud clusters use smaller overlapping irregular cards with reduced opacity.

## Verification

- `verify_face_river_sun_001.gd` checks bone-bound facial textures, bridge-safe route segments, sun geometry, apparent size, and sun/moon exclusivity.
- The standard runtime, story, visual, motion, river, packed-build, and Web checks remain mandatory.

## Remaining Limitation

Characters remain optimized stylized models. Facial identity is readable at conversation distance but is not cinematic facial capture or lip synchronization.
