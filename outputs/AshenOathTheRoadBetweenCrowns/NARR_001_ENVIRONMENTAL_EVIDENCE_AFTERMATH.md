# NARR-001 — Environmental Evidence and Aftermath

## Changes

- Every Road of Crows clue now records a named story-state flag alongside quest progress.
- Examined evidence receives a persistent grounded marker rather than simply vanishing.
- Wychwood pack victory restores tracks, ash residue, feathers, and broken binding whenever the zone is rebuilt.
- Greyfen's notice board and shrine reflect the chosen report method.
- The cemetery retains settled earth and a vigil lantern after its ambush.

## Running

Complete the Wychwood evidence and five-enemy fight, return to Greyfen to report, then revisit Wychwood. The post-fight trail and clearing residue remain visible after the transition.
