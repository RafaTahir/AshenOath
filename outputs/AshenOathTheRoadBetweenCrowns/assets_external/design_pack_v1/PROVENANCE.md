# Ashen Oath Design Pack v1

The user supplied `AshenOath_32_Asset_Design_Pack_v1.zip` and authorized its
32 designs for this game, with an additional Kael redesign in the same direction.
The archive contains concept art and design descriptions, not ready-made models.
No broader third-party redistribution license is asserted for those references.

The meshes, clothing, props, creature rigs and creature actions were authored
in Blender for this project. Human anatomy and the compatible humanoid skeleton
derive from the project's retained Quaternius Universal CC0 sources. Individual
source hashes, joint contracts and build records are in
`art_source/design_pack_v1/exports/` and `data/design_pack_assets.json`.
The retained human animation libraries keep their existing licenses/provenance.

Native Blender masters and reproducible authoring scripts are in
`art_source/design_pack_v1/`. Original source designs remain there as reference.
The new bodies replace runtime cast selections; environment, equipment, audio
and gameplay are not claimed to originate in this design pack.

Asset compilation and inclusion authorization are not runtime or artistic
acceptance. User review remains pending.
