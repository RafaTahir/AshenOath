Quaternius Ultimate Monsters
Original source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
Original public folder: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/18m4KpzpEzhC9wl7jzr6dUc0N8Jozr79C
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)

The original page identifies 50 fully animated monsters in FBX, OBJ, Blend,
and glTF formats and marks the pack CC0. Google Drive throttled the selected
files during recovery, so the self-contained glTF/GLB candidates were recovered
unchanged from public repositories that record the same creator, source pack,
and CC0 license:

- Demon.gltf, BlueDemon.gltf, Orc.gltf, Creep.glb
  Mirror: https://github.com/yerdaulet-damir/liminal
  Manifest: client/public/assets-manifest.json
- Ghost.glb
  Mirror: https://github.com/ilrein/warptracker
  Manifest: ASSETS.md

These files are audition candidates. Runtime approval requires Godot import,
animation, material, silhouette, performance, and gameplay evidence.
