# Revised opening interactive treatment

## Emotional shape

`help -> belong -> missing people -> contradiction -> preparation -> encounter -> evidence -> explicit report -> staying`

These are authoring durations, not measured completion. Rewritten subtitles are authoritative; old generated or recorded voice must not speak mismatched text.

## Threshold, approximately 0–8 minutes

A living cart driver is stuck outside Greyfen. Rook braces a wheel and jokes about an expensive push. The player helps through `greyfen_cart_work`, setting `cart_helped`. Anwen offers the driver a cup. Tor gives useful scrap. Greyfen receives somebody before its barred wartime gate becomes the argument.

The ordinary action offers warmth without resurrecting Bram, Sella, or Oren. Anwen keeps Oren's coat. Mira remembers the salve he needed after carving. Rook remembers Bram repairing his axle and charging once for two visits.

## The contract and road, approximately 8–15 minutes

The notice names the travelers. Anwen admits she tied the thread around recovered tokens; she tried a containment that failed. She does not recite the massacre. The questions are specific: why did the road repairs uncover named objects, and why were the missing travelers carrying them?

Follow the existing order-independent clues. Bram's tool shows he stayed to repair. Sella's thread shows shrine intervention. Oren's crow covers older wood and a restored name. Wire connects later patrol intervention. Drag marks show objects chosen over food. Minimum evidence advances; additional attention changes preparation.

## Encounter and aftermath, approximately 15–30 minutes

Prepare a route, trap, or oil. Ghoulkin gather incomplete identity-bearing testimony rather than delivering exposition. The aftermath retains objects and living interference. Kael's inference stays distinct from an observed fact: wire establishes a patrol's containment, not that Edric secretly ordered every attack.

## Return, approximately 30–45 minutes

Touching Anwen, the board, or retained evidence opens `report_decision`. It does not automatically pick a report. The three supported actions complete `main_road_of_crows:return_village` and set the report and Kael's pledge together.

- Private: give Anwen the original; stay to protect living carriers.
- Public: show the token and wire; stay with the people exposed by doing so.
- Retained: keep the original, make protected copies, and stay to understand the burden.

Kael: “I will stay. The payment ends the hunt. It does not settle what I now know.”

The choice has preview and cost text. Privacy buys preparation but delays the wider account. Publicity invites solidarity and exposure. Retention preserves leverage but leaves others waiting. The bell answers, and the altered village makes the decision perceptible.

## Cemetery, approximately 45–60 minutes

Anwen admits the novice's death, cut rope, and resealing. Three strokes now mean burial, restored name, and a promise no longer holding. Grave fragments deepen the same cause rather than starting another unrelated mystery. The chapel opens an actionable choice about redirected debt, memory, and temporary protection.

There is no cinematic control cut needed to make the next task meaningful. The person at the next location knows why Kael promised to return.
