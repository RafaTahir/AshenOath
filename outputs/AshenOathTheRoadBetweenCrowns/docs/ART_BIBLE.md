# Art Bible

## Retained Direction

Grounded stylized fantasy: believable scale, construction, weight, anatomy and
wear through retained optimized assets. This is not a mandate for photorealism,
new purchases or another replacement pass. Repair named defects, not endless taste
changes. Existing art, game content and visual settings remain.

## Identity and Silhouette

- Preserve Kael/Anwen's A-set identities, Senn's Ranger and the actual White Hart.
  Retain approved monster families and encounter dimensions.
- Roles read through proportions, posture, equipment and occupation, not palette
  swaps alone. Preserve sockets, skins, weighted anatomy and legitimate action poses.
- Regional palettes/named accents belong to `data/palettes.json` and existing
  silhouette/identity helpers. Preserve legitimate grey hair and pale clothing.
- Required surfaces retain authored materials/textures. Hit effects restore exact
  original bindings; shared identity materials remain immutable.
- Reuse retained facial work and actual capability bindings. Compilation is not
  visual approval of expression, anatomy or character appeal.

## Places and Readability

- Greyfen: supported stone/plaster/timber homes; care, kitchen, forge, warning bell,
  river and recognizable work.
- Wychwood: readable trails, territory and watercourse; dangerous is not uniformly evil.
- Deep Woods: machinery, freight, farmstead, orchard and mill show usefulness and
  its cost; preserve rescue routes and Ashwing's combat space.
- Long Road: crossing signals, marsh supports and checkpoint show real passage.
- Vargan: clear entrances, archives and evidence; keep essential proof reachable.
- Hartlands: retained Hart, ruins, memorials and living growth reflect the actual
  covenant and legitimate absence.

Ground props at believable scale. Keep routes, sightlines and interaction clearance
readable. Road landmarks and atlas names share the sector manifest. Fixtures are
interactive, reactive or clearly background, without competing prompts and floating
labels. Preserve bridge-only crossings and complete scenery before control.

## Materials, Light and Sound

Separate wood, stone, skin, cloth and metal through value, roughness and retained
textures. Oathfire cyan carries gameplay meaning, not arbitrary decoration.
Keep faces readable day/night without glowing actors or expensive new light farms.
Fog separates depth without erasing routes. Contact effects/sound follow actual
events; preserve subtitles, browser unlock and focus/pause recovery.

## Resource Discipline and Approval

Share immutable meshes, textures, rigs, clips and static batches. Preserve quality
presets and population; do not hide late actors or scenery to manufacture results.
Detailed nearby performances use existing scheduling. Keep licenses/provenance.
Unused source FBX/OBJ removal requires indirect dependency and export ownership
checks; inventory presence alone is not evidence of use.

User review supplies visual approval, listening and phone acceptance. Headless
checks cannot establish those qualities or numerical rendering performance.
