# A smaller cast, a remembered journey

Narrative direction and implementation map for the approved 32-design replacement. The canonical roster is the twelve supplied humans plus redesigned Kael; the twenty creatures cover existing encounters and ten additional regional observations. Existing quest IDs and earned outcomes remain compatibility anchors, not reasons to retain obsolete visible people. The chapter prose below guides existing scene revisions; it is not a claim that every described action has been observed in gameplay.

## The question that carries the book

Kael knows how to survive a road. Can he learn to stay responsible for the people who must use it after he leaves?

The lost escort is a recent personal failure; the massacre is an older institutional crime. They echo one another but never become the same event. Nera's survival does not excuse Kael. Vale's records do not give her Halvern's memories. The Hart knows the whole binding but cannot decide what anyone else must promise.

Each chapter begins with a recognizable person's practical need. A creature becomes relevant through its effect on that need, not a floating quest marker. The chapter closes with a visible change and a short remembered exchange. Quiet encounters, observation and nonviolent solutions sit alongside combat. Existing ending eligibility remains unchanged.

## Chapter I: The blanket is still yours

Greyfen introduces people through useful acts. Anwen hands out clean linen while asking Kael to leave enough room for the next patient. Nessa argues with Rook about the weight of a flour sack; the joke ends when she counts one household too many. Tor tests the warning mechanism with Kael holding a brace. Toma knows which field path is still firm; Elna brings an unoffered name packet and may take it home unopened. Mira distinguishes a familiar remedy from an uncertain inference.

Nera arrives with relief supplies. Her recognition of Kael waits until the load is safe. She is not introduced as a new quest dispenser beside an already identical arrival: her arrival replaces Maren's physical and narrative slot. Rook's food cart remains a different job.

The optional tutorial belongs to these actions. Nothing delays an immediate departure. The first road inquiry stays Anwen's missing travelers, with one clear northern destination. Kael can help without agreeing to a new promise.

## Chapter II: The wood is working

Toma's work route and Mira's water survey replace the disconnected forester/charcoal exposition. Their disagreement is concrete: where can a household gather fuel without ruining the run it drinks from? Rook's knowledge of an abandoned lookout is useful but incomplete. He does not become an eyewitness merely because the scene needs an explanation.

The existing first Wychwood encounter retains its population, phases and earned restoration until the new models and behavior are integrated. Stalker, Raider and Brute become recognizable tactical bodies within that encounter, not recolors. Bog Wretch remains the single authoritative capstone. The saved water choice still changes the bank arrangement and grants its current once-only reward.

## Chapter III: Work built over ash

Tor can repair machinery; he cannot repair a history by declaring it finished. Elna argues for workers who need a roof without agreeing that the mill was innocent. Toma's freight knowledge and Nera's relief crew replace separate paper couriers. Repairs, rescue and cultivation keep different rhythms.

Ashwing is heard and seen through smoke movement, a damaged perch and workers' attention before the fight. Rootbound protects a readable boundary, not an arbitrary stretch of walking ground. New creatures explain real water, heat and freight problems. A useful mill outcome does not erase the recorded responsibility.

## Chapter IV: Hold the rear

Nera knows how a crossing fails because she has brought people through one. Her six-chapter escort arc preserves Kael's confession, relief recovery, rear-guard work, account correction and homecoming. Merged dialogue is rewritten in her voice; old `side_escort_*` progress and reward keys stay earned.

Senn is introduced demonstrating competent passage control before defending its price. Rook distinguishes what he saw from what a supply ledger suggests. Neither replaces Senn's own admission. Custody, exile and execution remain separate authoritative outcomes. After resolution, Nera's arrangements and actual road access show what replaced his control.

## Chapter V: The price on the signature

Merrow deals with the next person in the queue before addressing Kael. Edric's useful supply system and coercive terms are visible together. Vale demonstrates exactly what an alteration proves, then tells Kael what it does not prove.

Archive assistant duties consolidate into Vale; service complaints can be carried by Elna or Nessa as their own witnessed concerns, not other people's invented experiences. Omitted detainee/gardener objectives need a rewritten accountable petition, not a silently renamed prisoner. Historical Halvern remains an attributed account and bounded memory. If his existing duel retains a visible memory body, it uses M04's Gravebound family; Vale never speaks in first person as Halvern.

No optional bargain, creature relocation or document-rescue activity can destroy the only guaranteed copy of essential proof.

## Chapter VI: What remains growing

Mira carries seeds because the place will still need tending. Tor carries a stone-working tool, not another speech about the oath. Their arrival is staged through the relief route; they are never duplicated in active village and glade scenes. Elna decides how much of her name packet belongs in a public memorial.

The Hart is approached through tracks, quiet attention and a complete recognizable body. Witness, Mercy, Duty and Ash remain legitimate, distinct outcomes. The optional creature encounters reveal land use and care, not extra ending keys.

Homecoming revisits the same people doing changed work. Nessa's table has a place to sit; Anwen's services remain freely available; Tor has something practical to finish. Nera may acknowledge cooperation without promising forgiveness. Kael's last reflection states what he chose, not what the author thinks he ought to feel.

The ten new regional inhabitants use authored idle/activity clips and an ordinary Observe interaction, with a description, a connection to the region's people, and a once-recorded journal entry. They grant no duplicate XP, invent no evidence and do not gate endings. The earlier design ideas for additional lure, eruption, latch and relocation mechanics are not part of this implementation.

## Adaptation and save cutover

1. Finish visual/animation production for a replacement before registering it. No image planes or renamed proxies count as the supplied creature.
2. Resolve an active logical role to one canonical actor and one location. Merge conversations at that actor, not by spawning another copy at every old anchor.
3. Keep old quest, choice, confession and reward keys. Reword their presentation only from the saved facts. Do not replay rewards or assume a fresh introduction has happened on an old save.
4. Separate statements an actor witnessed from accounts they read. Preserve denied consent and personal grievances when moving a scene's responsibility.
5. Map omitted service, companion and leisure access explicitly. Do not delete required dialogue, an unpaid reward or the player's equipment with an obsolete character model.
6. Only remove the old visible resources after all active, cached, prepared and fallback consumers use the replacements. Keep source provenance and a recoverable Git history.

The canonical roster, chapter topics, introductions, remembered accounts, creature body mappings and observation entries are implemented in source. Combined compilation and publication are recorded in PRODUCTION.md. Runtime appearance, performance, enjoyment and user acceptance remain unverified; the retained quests and existing encounter behavior are not newly certified by an asset export.
