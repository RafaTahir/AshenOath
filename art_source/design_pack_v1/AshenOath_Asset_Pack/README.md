# AshenOath - Creatures & Characters / Asset Design Pack 01

**32 original illustrated designs: 20 creatures and 12 established story characters.**

This is a finished **2D visual asset and 3D preproduction pack**. Each subject has its own PNG design sheet, detailed dossier, structured production data and exact image-generation prompt. Open `Open_Catalogue.html` after extracting the ZIP to browse and filter the collection offline. Open the PDF art book for a guided overview.

## What is included

- 20 individual monster sheets and 12 individual character sheets, supplied at their original generated resolution.
- Full-body and rear studies with portrait or anatomy/prop details. They are design references, not exact orthographic reconstructions.
- 32 individual dossiers covering visual identity, role, story purpose, relationships or encounters, and animation needs.
- `data/asset_manifest.json` with exact paths, dimensions, SHA-256 hashes and readiness status.
- Complete `data/monsters.json` and `data/characters.json`; `data/canon_audit.json` records repository grounding.
- `docs/Production_Handoff.md`, the production-oriented PDF, the offline catalogue, and all 32 original prompts.

## What these files are ready for

Art direction, visual review, bestiary/editorial use, character portraits within the illustrated sheets, and briefing a character artist or creature modeler. Extract the archive before opening the catalogue so relative image links resolve. Original sheets remain unchanged; the PDF only scales them for page layout.

**3D production remains to be done.** This pack does not include GLB/FBX meshes, UVs, baked PBR maps, rigs, skin weights, animation clips, collision, audio or tested Godot actor scenes. Images are not texture maps. The modeler must reconcile any cross-view differences before creating a mesh. No runtime performance or game acceptance is claimed.

## Canon and authorship

All 12 character identities exist in the inspected AshenOath repository. Ten monster identities already exist; ten are new additions proposed for this pack. Existing names, ages where documented, symbolic objects and major narrative constraints are retained. Faces, unspecified demographics, detailed anatomy and new future arcs are art-direction proposals. The dossiers label those distinctions.

The player's name is not changed. Missing/dead travelers are not resurrected. Halvern remains a bounded memory, and the White Hart remains the sole complete supernatural witness. New encounters do not automatically replace existing quest logic or save identifiers.

The artwork was generated individually using the built-in image-generation tool for this brief. No extracted Witcher assets or third-party character models are bundled. This pack does not assign a new license to the game's existing names, lore or source material. Generation provenance is recorded; no claim of hand-painted authorship is made.

## Using the pack with Codex or a modeler

1. Review the PNG for an actor and its matching dossier.
2. Read `docs/Production_Handoff.md` and the current project instructions.
3. Resolve the actor's actual rig, dimensions, collision, quest and animation contracts in the repository.
4. Build one source model, textures, rig and clips. Review all sides and deformations.
5. Export and integrate one actor in an isolated change, then play the relevant route and measure browser cost.
6. Keep the source art outside the runtime export. The game's existing release-size budget applies to the complete game, not this reference archive.

The visual pack is reviewable now; implementation and final art acceptance remain separate steps.
