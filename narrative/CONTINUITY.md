# People and the roads they keep

These are the decisions and changes to look for in the novel edition. Scene names refer to the four act files in the Godot project. SCENES.md gives the corresponding manuscript passages.

| Person | Where their story turns | What the game remembers |
|---|---|---|
| Kael | He can carry the broken cart account back under limits, admit how he left an escort eight years earlier, and choose who may hear the correction. In Open Road he takes the ferry back for Siv. | Evidence retains its provenance. He can decline the correction. Losing his own bow leaves his arrows intact. A borrowed or purchased replacement lets him shoot again. |
| Anwen | The brush and Harl’s bell put two different losses in her hands. Work at the fever bed continues after the story of the novice comes out. | Anwen’s work, her account, her consent to serve and any relocation are separate decisions. |
| Tor | The filed mark implicates his trade. Saving the wheelkeeper costs him the use of his left wrist. He teaches someone else to set the wedge. | The brace, work posture and supervised forge remember the accident. Spring gives him the joint to steady while Toma drives the peg. |
| Rook | A map protects households, but the relief schedule he passes along still exposes them. The culvert rescue costs him his breath and others’ trust. | The schedule and original map have separate histories. Food and treatment continue. Rook can send a bill from his shop and stay in Greyfen. |
| Mira | Pell’s family can question the origin of a treatment. At other times Mira can share bread, stop at the stairs, or make plans for the coast. | Treatment and intimacy have separate answers. A declared relationship permits the private scene. Refusal and friendship retain their own futures. |
| Elna | She lends Harl’s hollow bell, keeps or releases part of his story, and decides which personal things belong in her cupboard. | Returning the object preserves her custody independently of Kael’s record. Her grief supplies no automatic public testimony. |
| Toma | Pell’s drawing and the root beds give him a home life. Later three workers sign a petition under his hand. | The family’s treatment choice survives separately from the guardian’s fate. The garden’s workers and surrendered tools belong to the new edition. |
| Nessa | The price on the bakery receipt returns at the common kitchen. A burnt loaf and the need for cover make it possible for the founder to take an afternoon off. | The assigned shift, its relief and the kitchen’s continued work are distinct. |
| Nera | She chooses where to sit for Kael’s correction, whom she will speak to, and which routes her drivers will serve. | Six escort chapters keep their decisions. An evening’s company does not promise forgiveness, romance or another shift. |
| Senn | The inventory names what a seizure cost. A surviving Senn can give testimony and do supervised work behind a watched door. | Custody, survival, testimony and voluntary assent are distinct. Missing speakers have documentary fallbacks. |
| Edric | Bringing broth precedes the bargain. The seals allow comparison of a thirty-two-year order and his later suppression. Grain can be issued without another compelled oath. | The recovered documents survive pressure or exposure. Compulsion cannot create a willing witness. |
| Vale | A splinter, an unfinished game and a badly judged inference make room for a life beyond the archive. She handles both copies and originals. | A private account stays protected. The player’s relationship with Vale may differ from the novel’s friendship. She can mark an inference as uncertain. |
| Merrow | His inspection counterpart bears his own signature. A borrowed key can reveal the narrower refusal record. The later stores check is supervised. | Finding the duplicate is optional. It neither proves the older massacre nor restores Merrow’s authority. |

## The six springs

| Enacted covenant | Work that survives it |
|---|---|
| Witness | Prepare a public surface that people can reach and distinguish the public copy from protected testimony. |
| Mercy | Return to a name kept in safe custody and decide what a small circle can carry. |
| Duty | Tend the boundary and reckon with the repeated labor of keeping a watch. |
| Ash | Return to the ground left after the fight. The human accounts survive without another hearing from the Hart. |
| Concord | Share a shift that someone may leave. The rota retains the distinction between the original agreement and present service. |
| Open Road | Move the chair through the freight door, steady it for Siv’s father and receive new bedding. Bera’s and Siv’s crossings, the lost cargo and the vanished territorial protection stay in the account. |

The six-month visit requires an explicit choice after the covenant is enacted. Earlier chapters can be played with different evidence, relationships and permissions. Sella’s prologue belongs to her point of view. The Hart witnessed the old gate and cannot settle what happened on Kael’s escort.
