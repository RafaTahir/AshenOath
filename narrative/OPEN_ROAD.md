## Shelter and passage

The late evacuation carries two households. Bera brings her daughter and her
older and younger grandchildren. Siv brings her father and sister. Bera and
Siv are the only two with personal names. The five relatives use relationship
labels. Their own families remained home when Bera and Siv helped prepare the
refuge for the earlier arrivals (novel lines 8757, 10178–10186 and 10845–10853).

Vale reads back the requests for shelter, beds, somewhere to dry boots and help
carrying Siv's father. Household members confirm their wishes. The shelter
record and the existing keeper, testimony, custody and relationship choices
are independent (novel lines 10061–10063 and 10166–10196, companion lines 87–93).

## Crossing, care and handover

1. The glade ritual dismantles the ward and ends access to the Hart's complete
   account. The human copies stay protected. At Lantern Reach the player reads
   back the two requests and prepares the rescue. Flood debris takes the
   upstream guide and the separate hauling line survives. The game shows the
   changed rig and represents the earlier setup trip offscreen
   (novel lines 10166–10196, 10386–10434 and 10475–10493).
2. Kael rides the ferry with the ferryman and the current passenger group.
   The two prepared character roles supply shared static poses for the rescue
   cast. Competing bridge guidance gives way to the active landing while the
   saved community route choice is retained. A completed leg records its
   landing. Interrupted legs return Kael and the ferry to their departure bank.
3. After the return, the second-rescue station records Kael's calf wound and
   bow loss. Siv steadies her father while Rook and Siv's sister help him down.
   The injury and equipment loss are recorded story events, represented at the
   station with no combat simulation or inventory deduction
   (novel lines 10603–10715).
4. East-bank delivery counts seven and records Siv binding the wound and Anwen
   cleaning it at the overnight shelter. The goods account records the chair,
   bedding chest, two sacks of grain and much of the last tool load. The
   overnight stay is represented in the delivery account
   (novel lines 10721, 10779–10789 and 10801–10833).
5. The Hearthward work station confirms all seven indoors. Nera marks the
   arrival, Mira puts in twelve stitches, Anwen helps with care, and Nessa
   arranges food and beds (novel lines 10845–10853 and 10874–10946).
6. The later Hearthward handover confirms the ferry crew for Greyfen and the
   mill run with Siv and Toma. Nera takes the outlying shelters with two
   drivers. Kael takes a seated stores check with Merrow and Vale checks after
   them. The station publishes the handover before the ending can finish.
   The companion places the separate companionship scene after covenant
   completion (novel lines 10980–11010 and 11076–11152, companion lines 59–63
   and 87–93).

## Compatibility and checks

The new route applies to Open Road journeys with numeric `novel_edition=1`.
Existing journeys keep their original progression. Continue uses the existing
story flags and save cancellation. Save snapshots taken during ferry movement
start the player back at the departure dock with committed progress intact.

The Godot 4.6.3 headless model and ferry fixtures cover sequence and capacity,
across-river and Hearthward counts, delivery and handover gates, interrupted
travel, save round-trips, guide persistence, and legacy progression. The ferry
fixture uses placeholder meshes. Rendered actor placement and a full campaign
playthrough remain unverified.
