## Shared Contract

- Priority: pause/death, protected interaction, combat/immediate danger, social,
  routine, idle. Existing dialogue suspends combat; no competing action owner.
- Existing profiles, animation/face drivers, routines, spatial service and
  StoryState remain authoritative. No parallel affection, quest or AI framework.
- Named humans have an occupation, concern, social links, mannerism and permitted
  reaction. Actual facial joints/mesh bindings establish capability; naming a
  surface or setting a metadata boolean cannot establish a functioning face.
- Use registered actors, bounded sight queries and staggered decisions. Close
  facial work and hand posing sleep at distance; bodies/population remain.
- Save significant known events using existing state. Do not store blinks,
  fabricate past interactions, duplicate rewards or infer private knowledge.
- Preserve Kael/Anwen identity, Ranger Senn, the actual White Hart, controls,
  equipment, encounters, settings, saves, world content and complete readiness.
- No paid assets, new crime/death system, generative online dialogue or compulsory
  synthetic/recorded voice production. Retain approved speech/subtitle policy.
- All project and outputs stay on D:. Whole deployment, including its manifest,
  is strictly below 104857600 bytes. No content deletion or quality downgrade.
- Record implementation, compilation, observations and user acceptance separately.
