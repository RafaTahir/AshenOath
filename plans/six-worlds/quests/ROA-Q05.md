# ROA-Q05 - The Road Belongs to No One

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-104](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-104.md). Runtime ID: `saga_roa_q05`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional connected aftermath. XP: 500. Any existing settled Senn fate.

## Hook And People

The checkpoint is no longer Senn's unresolved confrontation, but a road still
needs maintenance, rate information and somewhere to report abuse. Nera and
Tavin want a shared practice rather than Kael declaring himself its new owner.
Nera: "Taking down a name does not keep the boards above the mud."

## Playable Objectives

1. `inspect_vacated_control`: inspect the surrendered/abandoned command table and
   supplies. Describe the actual custody, exile or death outcome without inventing consent.
2. `hear_maintenance`: compare Nera's keeper rota and Tavin's written supply plan.
3. `choose_passage`: establish a public maintenance ledger with rotating volunteers,
   or a keeper-run notice board with open inspection of every charge.
4. `open_lane`: remove the obsolete toll beam/loose barricade from the already
   walkable corridor and install the chosen board on its shoulder.
5. `make_handover`: set a supply tray and post that aid and passage do not require
   signing a military oath. Observe a traveler use the clear lane through real movement.

## Branches And Consequences

`shared_rota`: voluntary names and ordinary repair shifts. `open_keeper`: one
responsible keeper with publicly visible charges. Both permit refusal and passage.
Do not call an execution peaceful or place living Senn in exile/death aftermath.
The later Assembly can cite the arrangement, not assume every traveler agreed to a covenant.

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save plan, beam retired, board/tray and `saga_roa_q05_outcome`
(`shared_rota`, `open_keeper`). Reward: 500 XP once, distinct from capstone XP/baldric.
After leaving/returning inspect the board for `return:saga_roa_settled` (100 XP).
Late saves use remaining volunteers/written plans. Player absence pauses the
demonstration traveler at a safe point; no route teleport, random casualty or confiscation.
Q01-Q04 are not required. No new toll purchase or claim that the whole war is settled.
