# WYC-Q04 - The Hunter's Blind

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-102](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-102.md). Runtime ID: `saga_wyc_q04`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional exploratory story. XP: 300. Starts by reading an exposed trail mark.

## Hook And People

An old hunter marked a blind for a relative who never learned the route. Iven
remembers the dispute over whether the hunter disappeared or simply left the work.
A mundane private departure can be true without becoming another massacre clue.
The note: "If you found this, the marks still work better than my directions."

## Playable Objectives

1. `read_first_mark`: inspect a visible split-feather sign near the southern deadfall.
2. `follow_three_marks`: find three carved branch scars along clear ground paths;
   order may vary, the journal retains each observed direction.
3. `open_blind`: move the ground-level blind's loose screen and read its maintenance
   diary. No tower climb or new traversal ability is required.
4. `deliver_deposit`: take the owner's labeled tool deposit to Iven's worker table,
   or preserve it in the blind and post its whereabouts with private details removed.

## Branches And Consequences

`returned`: the tools rejoin maintained work. `preserved`: the blind remains an
interpretable place with the private diary undisturbed. Both record an agreed account.
WYC-S01 is a distinct optional dry-pocket cache hinted by the blind carving;
finding the quest's tools does not automatically discover/claim that secret.
Iven: "So he left a way to be found. That is more than we knew."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save mark IDs, diary read, deposit custody and `saga_wyc_q04_outcome`
(`returned`, `preserved`). Reward: 300 XP and one iron trap once.
Tools cannot be sold or lost. A written deposit record completes the handover
if Iven is absent. Mark visibility works day/night and does not require the
compass to reveal unearned exact coordinates. No original clue is removed.

## User Manual Checklist

Follow marks out of order and at night, reload holding the deposit, take both
resolutions and independently claim the neighboring secret without double rewards.
