# HAR-Q05 - What We Leave Growing

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-106](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-106.md). Runtime ID: `saga_har_q05`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional connected aftermath. XP: 500. `final_choice_completed` true.

## Hook And People

The covenant has its actual chosen outcome; the living still decide where to
place tools, shelter and future care. Maelin and Oris offer a finite day of work,
not a second ending referendum. Their earlier quests are not prerequisites.
Maelin: "We can leave the place better tended without deciding for everyone who comes next."

## Playable Objectives

1. `survey_aftermath`: inspect the original covenant-specific garden and a small
   adjacent work plot outside the primary memory/stone composition.
2. `choose_tending`: choose a shared nursery with voluntary named custodians,
   or an open resting shelter with a public maintenance record and no binding pledge.
3. `prepare_plot`: carry two supplied pieces, clear loose hazards and fit a
   shallow tray or bench at authored sockets. Preserve all original aftermath meshes.
4. `place_record`: copy the actual covenant's known consequences with attribution;
   offer space for disagreement, never claim the whole village consented.
5. `make_handover`: leave tools/maintenance instructions and take the ordinary
   walk back to the branch marker. This is distinct from the retained Greyfen homecoming.

## Branches And Consequences

`nursery`/`resting_shelter` work in all covenants. Witness keeps accessible copies;
Mercy does not republish intimate names; Duty describes only Kael's stewardship;
Ash offers unbound ordinary care, not regeneration of the destroyed covenant.
Small living growth or shelter use remains plausible in the adjacent safe plot.
Oris: "We know what we did today. Tomorrow someone else can decide to join us."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save survey, tending choice, piece count, attributed record and
`saga_har_q05_outcome` (`nursery`, `resting_shelter`), plus the actual covenant key.
Reward: 500 XP once. Existing final-resolution XP/inlay remain separate entitlements.
On a later sector return, inspect the tended plot for `return:saga_har_settled` (100 XP).
Standing offered plans handle absent visitors without forged signatures. All
old endings/Anwen/workbench/road reflections stay complete or available; do not
reset `final_choice_completed`, respawn the avatar or repeat the final choice.

## User Manual Checklist

Finish both tending plans under all four endings, reload mid-build, revisit
twice and complete/retain the original full Greyfen return-home epilogue afterward.
