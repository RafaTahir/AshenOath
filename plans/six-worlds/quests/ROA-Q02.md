# ROA-Q02 - The Crossing Keeper's Oath

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-104](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-104.md). Runtime ID: `saga_roa_q02`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected personal responsibility. XP: 350. Meet Nera/read closure marker.

## Hook And People

Nera once kept a warning lantern dark under a closure order while someone called
from the far bank. She cannot prove the caller's fate. She wants a search and an
honest safety practice, not Kael announcing a death or forgiveness on no evidence.
Nera: "I can tell you what I did. I cannot tell you what happened beyond the reeds."

## Playable Objectives

1. `hear_closure`: hear the bounded account or read her signed keeper's note.
2. `inspect_last_marks`: inspect the old mooring post, cut cord and a dated
   shore tally from a safe existing bank. Nothing requires swimming.
3. `recover_work_record`: open the ground-level keeper's box: a later rescue
   crew recorded finding the abandoned craft, not identifying its passenger.
4. `choose_account`: lodge Nera's admission in the open crossing log, or preserve
   protected copies with an invitation for affected families to answer voluntarily.
5. `set_rescue_terms`: place a clear warning board and emergency rope at the
   existing legal crossing; practice the release while standing on dry support.

## Branches And Consequences

`open_log`: the keeper's role is public. `protected`: the factual account is
preserved without identifying possible relatives. Neither invents a survivor,
death, ghost witness or permission to repeat the closure. The new emergency
practice appears on return, not merely a forgiveness dialogue line.
Kael: "We can leave a rope where an order once stood. We still keep the account."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save observations, copy custody, rope/board and `saga_roa_q02_outcome`
(`open_log`, `protected`). Reward: 350 XP and one redroot potion once.
Nera's absent-state written instructions support physical safety work; personal
acknowledgement waits. No original command proof or route unlock depends on this.
Reload restores a secured rope; no player/collider on the forbidden water surface.

## User Manual Checklist

Take both disclosure routes, work with Nera absent, interrupt rope placement
and confirm no fabricated witness, new water crossing or main-story gate.
