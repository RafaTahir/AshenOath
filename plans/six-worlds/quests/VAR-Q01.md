# VAR-Q01 - The Orders Between Lines

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-105](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-105.md). Runtime ID: `saga_var_q01`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected investigation. XP: 300. Record Hall accessible.

## Hook And People

Lysa has been ordered to recopy a current supply instruction without its earlier
margin. She wants the alteration recorded without being blamed for writing the
order herself. Vale can distinguish a clerk's hand from the authority of its seal.
Lysa: "I copied the words. That does not mean I chose which ones disappeared."

## Playable Objectives

1. `hear_alteration`: accept Lysa's comparison task or read her offered work note.
2. `compare_copies`: inspect original margin, revised working sheet and outgoing
   register entry on three accessible archive desks, in any order.
3. `attribute_hands`: use dated countersigns to separate author, copier and approver;
   mark uncertain authorship explicitly instead of inventing a guilty servant.
4. `choose_custody`: lodge redacted copies in Vale's public archive, or retain
   protected duplicates for the existing public hearing.
5. `file_comparison`: physically arrange the comparison under the chosen heading
   and let Lysa approve the description of her part, not swear to its conclusion.

## Branches And Consequences

`archive`: comparison visible without household addresses. `protected`: sealed
duplicates are available at the hearing. Both add context to current renewal
administration; neither substitutes for or can destroy guaranteed command proof.
Lysa receives an outcome-specific work line, not a mandatory romantic scene.
Vale: "A signature tells us who signed. It is a beginning, not a whole account."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save three comparison facts, attribution, lodged copies and `saga_var_q01_outcome`
(`archive`, `protected`). Reward: 300 XP and a comparison dossier lore entry.
Works after any original ledger choice and after Edric loses power; tense changes.
Lysa's signed note and archival drop allow completion when actors are absent.
Interrupted arrangement resumes remaining sheets, with no new generic discovery XP
for re-reading the same page and no forced repeat of the archive haunting.

## User Manual Checklist

Compare copies in different orders, choose both custodians, use each old ledger
disposition, reload midway and confirm guaranteed proof/haunting state remains intact.
