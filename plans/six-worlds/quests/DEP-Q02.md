# DEP-Q02 - A Field Without Ash

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-103](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-103.md). Runtime ID: `saga_dep_q02`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected practical recovery. XP: 350. Farmstead access only.

## Hook And People

Ressa wants a planting bed whose origin she can explain to a neighbor. She cannot
feed a village with a demonstration plot, but refuses the claim that nothing
can be done unless the tainted channels keep running forever.
Ressa: "Not enough for everyone. Enough to learn what next year might take."

## Playable Objectives

1. `survey_plot`: inspect a cleared bed and its old runoff edge with Ressa.
2. `source_clean_fill`: compare clean compost beside the public work pile with
   stored ash soil. Select the clean material; do not present unsafe fill as equal.
3. `choose_layout`: choose a raised contained bed or a screened terrace using
   freely loaned sacks/boards. Both keep the existing through-route clear.
4. `prepare_bed`: carry two quest-work loads, position their edging and rake the bed.
5. `plant_trial`: set Ressa's supplied seed tray and label its origin; record a
   plan to observe, not an instantly grown harvest or a guaranteed cure for famine.

## Branches And Consequences

`raised`: accessible contained trial near the yard; `terrace`: more drainage
maintenance and a larger small bed. Both remain independent of the mill's selected future.
After a later existing chapter milestone, show modest shoots, not overnight full grain.
Nessa/Toma may acknowledge a possible future supply without claiming a solved food crisis.
Kael: "Then we should write down what we used, including what did not work."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save survey, fill loads, layout, planted state and `saga_dep_q02_outcome`
(`raised`, `terrace`). Reward: 350 XP and two redroot ingredients once.
No purchase, rare herb or real-time wait required. Written work plan is available
if Ressa is absent; the last seed tray is protected quest work, not food inventory.
Paused/partial jobs reconstruct the remaining loads without growing another free harvest.
No changes to the existing sacrifice-root decision, crop yield economy or global weather.

## User Manual Checklist

Use both layouts, interrupt with one load placed, reload after planting, return
after a chapter milestone and confirm no instant harvest or main-quest gate.
