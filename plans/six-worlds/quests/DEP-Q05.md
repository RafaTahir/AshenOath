# DEP-Q05 - A Roof Before Rain

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-103](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-103.md). Runtime ID: `saga_dep_q05`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional connected aftermath. XP: 500. Ashwing resolved; existing mill operation decided.
Legacy exception: a completed original mill chapter with an unknown operation
permits the detached workshop without inventing/reopening the original choice.

## Hook And People

Dain needs a dry place for workers and honest accounts regardless of whether
the machinery runs. Ressa offers knowledge of reusable roof pieces, not forgiveness
for the mill. This begins after the old rescue/wage/operation decisions, not instead of them.
Dain: "A closed mill still has people outside it. They get wet in the same rain."

## Playable Objectives

1. `survey_shelter`: inspect the storm-damaged yard cover and two usable supports.
2. `choose_station`: for `closed`, choose a relief store or detached workers'
   workshop; for `supervised`/`restitution`, choose a dry checking station or
   detached workshop. These local choices never change the existing mill operation.
   A legacy unknown operation permits only the workshop, preserving that unknown state.
3. `fit_cover`: carry three freely supplied sound work pieces, peg the frame and
   lay the cover. Keep combat/return lanes and the rescue channel clear.
4. `move_current_records`: put the new work rota/dispatch copies under cover;
   do not silently mark the original wage chest rescued.
5. `open_workplace`: inspect the finished shelter with Dain or his posted handover.

## Branches And Consequences

`relief_store`, `checking_station`, `workshop` record visible useful futures.
All preserve the earlier ledger/operation/rescue outcomes and workers' right
to refuse the work. Greyfen's later supply dialogue acknowledges the actual choice.
Ressa: "A roof is not an answer to everything. It is an answer to rain."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save supports, piece count, records placed and `saga_dep_q05_outcome` from the
three named values. Reward: 500 XP once; no construction purchase or weather timer.
The 100-XP settled return is a later visit to this changed station. Ashwing XP
and fitting entitlement stay with the old encounter, not this construction job.
The fitting's duration perk remains explicitly blocked by the user's oil-system
deferral; this quest neither invents a timer nor substitutes another effect.
Advanced saves can start from resolved Ashwing; absent Dain uses his work plan.

## User Manual Checklist

Use all old mill operations and compatible shelter choices, reload mid-frame,
verify original rescue flags unchanged and inspect the separate blocked-perk status.
