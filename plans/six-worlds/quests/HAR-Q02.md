# HAR-Q02 - A Promise Made in Fear

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-106](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-106.md). Runtime ID: `saga_har_q02`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected personal story. XP: 350. Oris's voluntary conversation available.

## Hook And People

Oris promised never to leave the memorial until every missing person had a name.
Nobody who needed his work asked for that impossible vow. He now fears that
stopping means abandoning them. Kael recognizes the temptation to promise beyond
what one person can do, without equating this with his own earlier retreat.
Oris: "I said 'every name.' It was the first thing I had said that sounded large enough."

## Playable Objectives

1. `hear_promise`: let Oris describe his own promise and the work he cannot finish.
2. `separate_obligations`: inspect his unfinished commission, written voluntary
   request and self-added pledge. Distinguish paid work, care and self-punishment.
3. `offer_terms`: offer withdrawal without another promise, or help him define
   a finite commission with a named end. Oris explicitly accepts/refuses for himself.
4. `finish_finite_work`: stack the already-cut plaques in an agreed dry rack;
   if withdrawing, prepare their handover rather than abandoning them in the rain.
5. `record_his_answer`: Oris marks his chosen release or limited responsibility
   and physically leaves the vigil position for a rest/work place.

## Branches And Consequences

`released`: no replacement vow, work handed over safely. `bounded`: a freely
chosen finite job with time to rest. Neither requires a magical consent ritual,
forces Duty, or proves fear-made promises were legitimate bindings.
Kael: "You can answer for the stones you cut. You do not owe the world every name."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save facts, accepted finite task, rack progress and `saga_har_q02_outcome`
(`released`, `bounded`). Reward: 350 XP and one redroot potion once.
If Oris is absent before choosing, work may be inspected/prepared but the personal
decision waits; no page forges his answer. Existing ending remains freely available.
No dialogue approval farming, forced new oath or count-all-artifacts prerequisite.
Interrupted rack work resumes and actor staging never duplicates Oris.

## User Manual Checklist

Decline/return to conversation, take each freely accepted outcome, interrupt
physical work and choose each main ending independently of Oris's promise.
