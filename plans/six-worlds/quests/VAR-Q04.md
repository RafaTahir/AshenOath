# VAR-Q04 - A Seal Without a Crown

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-105](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-105.md). Runtime ID: `saga_var_q04`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional civic/exploration story. XP: 300. Existing ledger disposition recorded.

## Hook And People

Merrow can issue grain only through forms that still carry household assent
beside the seal. Lysa knows the paper trail; Vale wants an account that can be
challenged rather than another perfect-looking command.
Merrow: "An empty seal does not feed anybody. A dishonest one does not help for long."

## Playable Objectives

1. `compare_warrants`: inspect a blank supply warrant, current ration condition
   and old warehouse receipt. This is administrative repair, not new proof of the massacre.
2. `trace_authority`: follow matching docket numbers between courtyard store
   and archive intake using existing paths/service access.
3. `choose_register`: choose an openly inspectable distribution register or
   protected household receipts with public aggregate totals. Both remove assent.
4. `prepare_clean_forms`: strike the coercive clause on new duplicate forms,
   stamp them with the dated supply authority and mark both custodians.
5. `issue_trial`: authorize one ordinary supply parcel and place its matching
   receipt at the store. No real food or gold spent by Kael.

## Branches And Consequences

`public_totals`: visible named voluntary officers and aggregate allocations.
`private_receipts`: households keep their own copies, totals remain challengeable.
This provides a practical alternative the Assembly can acknowledge; it does not
silently finish the existing renewal-stopping objective or force Edric's stance.
Vale: "Leave a place for someone to write that we were wrong."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save docket facts, clause/form states, issued parcel and `saga_var_q04_outcome`
(`public_totals`, `private_receipts`). Reward: 300 XP and clean-warrant lore record.
If Merrow/Edric is absent, documented store authority and Vale's intake complete
the transaction without pretending a departed lord consented. After the Assembly,
describe this as operational follow-through, not reversal of its achieved freedom.
Reload cannot issue a second reward parcel or duplicate quest XP.

## User Manual Checklist

Use both registers before/after the Assembly, with each ledger/Edric outcome;
reload after stamping and confirm independent original renewal progression.
