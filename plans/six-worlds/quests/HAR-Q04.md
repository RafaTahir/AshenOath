# HAR-Q04 - The Keeper's Last Seed

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-106](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-106.md). Runtime ID: `saga_har_q04`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional independent conservation story. XP: 300. Maelin's offered reserve accessible.

## Hook And People

Maelin has one working seed reserve from a threatened local plant, not the last
seed of a world-saving magical species. She wants a viable next season and fears
losing the whole reserve to one hopeful planting.
Maelin: "A seed is not a promise. It needs somewhere to fail without taking the rest."

## Playable Objectives

1. `inspect_reserve`: examine her offered seed envelope and growing notes with consent.
2. `survey_sites`: compare a drained Ruins alcove and a sheltered woodland bed;
   use visible soil/water clues, not a specific time of day or secret collection.
3. `choose_plan`: split a trial portion between both sites, or use the better
   sheltered site and keep a labeled dry reserve for a future attempt.
4. `set_trial`: carry supplied soil cups, place them at the selected sockets and
   label their origin. Never consume seeds from a secret or earlier regional quest.
5. `store_reserve`: place the agreed reserve/record in Maelin's dry box and receive
   her planting plan; completion is preparation, not instant full growth.

## Branches And Consequences

`split_trial`: two modest marked cups. `reserve_first`: one larger trial and a
dry reserve. A later story milestone can show small shoots in valid sheltered
places. No guaranteed restoration or demand to visit all six worlds again.
Ending changes the surrounding context, not the legitimacy of her practical work.

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save both surveys, cup IDs, stored reserve and `saga_har_q04_outcome`
(`split_trial`, `reserve_first`). Reward: 300 XP and two grave_moss ingredients once.
Maelin's standing written work plan allows careful planting if she is absent;
private seed notes are not published without her choice. No paid supplies,
irrecoverable rare item, daily farming loop or skill/tree-gate introduced.
Reload reconstructs reserve and trials from one saved allocation, never extra seed rewards.

## User Manual Checklist

Try both plans, reload while carrying a soil cup, finish with no regional
secrets collected and confirm no instant growth or new prerequisite for an ending.
