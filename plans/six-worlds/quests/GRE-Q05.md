# GRE-Q05 - A Gate Left Open

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-101](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-101.md). Runtime ID: `saga_gre_q05`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected aftermath. XP: 500. Q01-Q04 are not prerequisites.

## Hook And People

After Bell-Eater's defeat, two exhausted travelers wait outside the village
threshold. Nessa offers a dry store corner; Jory wants an agreed warning so the
watch does not mistake their arrival for another attack. Anwen refuses a new oath
as the price of a bed. These are living visitors, not replacement missing travelers.
Anwen: "The bell is quiet. Now we decide whether that means they may come in."

## Playable Objectives

1. `meet_arrivals`: greet the travelers at the safe road verge after the existing
   boss outcome, or the explicit grandfathered legacy shrine retirement state.
2. `prepare_shelter`: inspect two existing usable spaces; open Nessa's dry store
   corner or arrange a quieter courtyard room with the household's permission.
3. `escort_to_shelter`: walk the short village route with them. They wait safely
   when Kael leaves; no combat or timer is required to preserve their lives.
4. `post_terms`: place a plain admission notice: shelter without signature, plus
   the agreed warning instructions. Speak with the travelers after they settle.

## Branches And Consequences

`store`: communal evening company and a screen for privacy. `courtyard`: quieter
space and an agreed morning check-in. Both are valid sanctuary, not good/evil scores.
The next return shows bedding actually used and two distinct routines. A later
Assembly reference is available but cannot count them as consenting witnesses.
Traveler: "Tell me the way out as well. Then I can choose to stay."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save chosen space, escort progress/safe anchor, admission posted and
`saga_gre_q05_outcome` (`store`, `courtyard`). Reward: 500 XP once; no supplies removed.
Bellkeeper's Clasp and retained boss XP belong to capstone resolution, not this quest.
Leaving then returning to the new shelter enables the separate 100-XP settled return.
If Nessa/Jory is absent, their standing offers and watch board support the work.
Advanced saves can complete it without replaying Bell-Eater or choosing another shrine fate.
Keep this shelter distinct from the original household relief deliveries/homecoming.

## User Manual Checklist

Use both shelters, leave/reload midway through the escort, finish on a legacy save,
return twice and complete the original Assembly and homecoming afterward.
