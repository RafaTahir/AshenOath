# DEP-Q01 - The Second Ledger

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-103](../worlds/WORLD-SAGA-103.md). Runtime ID: `saga_dep_q01`.
Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending. See [current build/delivery record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Type: optional, connected. XP: 300. Start at the mill's exterior dispatch notebook.

## Hook And People

Dain found that current sacks disappear between the mill and the military road.
He needs the workers' present labor distinguished from the atrocity in the old
mill account. The second book records today's freight, not another lost command proof.
Dain: "The old book tells you what was done. This one tells you who still gets paid."

## Playable Objectives

1. `read_dispatch`: inspect a current dispatch page at the exterior safe work table.
2. `compare_sack_marks`: compare three current sack seals with wheelbarrow scrape
   marks and a short manifest; finding either first preserves the observations.
3. `trace_fee`: follow the marked yard route to a rejected-load slip. The levy
   redirects supplies through the checkpoint; a worker also concealed an emergency share.
4. `settle_account`: choose a public, address-redacted freight comparison or a
   workers' witnessed account lodged with the regional supply clerk.
5. `mark_next_load`: re-tag an actual new dispatch set and place the chosen copy
   in the outbound box. Do not complete by opening the original wage chest.

## Branches And Consequences

`public`: clean current figures on the yard board. `witnessed`: duplicate worker
copies without household details. Both can inform ROA-Q01, never automatically finish it.
No accusation of original massacre command is inferred from a current levy.
Rescued workers later refer to the record they now control.
Dain: "At least the next sack will have a name we can answer for."

## State, Rewards And Recovery

Save compared seals, source facts, dispatch tags and `saga_dep_q01_outcome`
(`public`, `witnessed`). Reward: 300 XP and one travel bread once.
Before Dain is safe, his signed notebook carries the hook and approved lodgement;
completion need not force him to talk inside a burning combat encounter.
All three old mill-operation choices allow a final dispatch/return-of-stock record.
Quest book is not sellable, consumable or the existing wage/miller ledger item.
Reload reuses already-tagged sacks and leaves original rescue progress unchanged.

## User Manual Checklist

Start before/after worker rescue, read clues in different orders, finish with
closed/supervised/restitution mill states and confirm distinct old/new ledger rewards.
