## People And Regional Story

Edric's authority is enacted by ordinary hands. Vale must protect people as well
as records; Merrow knows what keeping the supplies moving costs. Lysa, an archive
assistant, notices alterations but fears becoming the convenient culprit. Odran,
a detained gardener, wants his tools and freedom to refuse work, not a heroic title.
Keep the servant, guards, Vale's existing personal/romance arc and Halvern's bounded memory.

Stage Lysa in the existing archive work aisle and Odran at a small courtyard
service/garden alcove. Use retained architecture and safe approaches, not a
new prison complex or packed NPCs in the gate. Their private notes can remain
when absent; never pretend a written page is a live speaker.

  Contents: an archivist's ordinary household inventory, six Standard arrows

- [ ] VAR-S03, gardener's seed collection. A plant label cross-references the

  standing written invitation; personal seeds are recorded and left, not stolen.

Odran's current working seed tray in VAR-Q03 is separate from the secret collection.
Consent to inspect is not consent to publish an address or promise for a household.
