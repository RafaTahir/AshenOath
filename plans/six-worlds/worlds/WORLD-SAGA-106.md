# WORLD-SAGA-106 - The Hartlands: What We Leave Growing

Parent: [WORLD-SAGA-000](../WORLD-SAGA-000.md). Status: build-only implementation present; user acceptance pending.
Current compilation/publication details: [implementation record](../IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Region: `hartlands`. Districts: retained Old Ruins and White Hart Glade.

## People And Regional Story

Maelin is a living seed keeper carrying a modest reserve, not a new keeper of
all supernatural truth. Oris is a living stoneworker who promised more memorial
work than fear allowed him to refuse. They arrive by the reopened road; neither
was secretly present at the massacre or replaces the Hart's complete witness.
Give each a sheltered work place in the Ruins and respectful space from the Glade.

Make Old Ruins a reachable late optional district: a clear woodland track joins
the Glade's western shoulder and the Ruins' eastern arrival through the existing
sector/arrival service. Register an additive branch edge and safe anchors;
preserve saved sector IDs/coordinates and existing return links, not a new world engine.
No mandatory exterior portal/extra loading overlay. The existing Assembly-to-Glade
path stays primary. Legacy Edric/Hart shortcut staging is phase-controlled so
the new branch never duplicates a current speaker or replays a resolved encounter.

## Artifacts And Secrets

- [ ] HAR-S01, overgrown sanctuary. Weathered reliefs at a Ruins wall line up
  with a distinct fallen lintel; clear the loose vine screen and inspect its dry
  interior. Contents: a limited record of ordinary sanctuary work, 100 XP.
  It neither identifies every victim nor overrides the complete Hart testimony.
- [ ] HAR-S02, unfinished stoneworker's memorial. Follow a chisel-maker's mark
  from a discarded practice tile to a shallow, safe work niche. Contents:
  an unfinished maker's dedication and one iron trap in a current work crate,
  100 XP. Distinct from Oris's new current plaques and VAR-Q05's repository.
- [ ] HAR-S03, changed-covenant grove. After the final choice, each aftermath
  variant exposes the same shallow old root passage: opened canopy for Witness,
  unbound ribbons for Mercy, a steward marker for Duty, scorched screen for Ash.
  Contents: a seasonal-growth record and 100 XP. All variants remain accessible;
  no hidden superior ending, resurrection or new compulsory oath.
