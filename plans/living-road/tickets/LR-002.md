## Complete Implementation

Bind both to validated back/spine bones and a shared hand socket; only the selected drawn blade enters the hand. Add 3 for Oathblade while preserving 1 Steel and 2 Bow; controller cycle visits all three. Expose remapping through the existing settings model. Draw state remains under EquipmentLoadout, not duplicate controller booleans.

## Persistence And Interruptions

Additive save fields default old sword saves to Steel; preserve inventory, oil, arrow type, remaps and quests. Unknown blade IDs recover to Steel. Empty or interrupted equipment states must resolve to one active weapon.
