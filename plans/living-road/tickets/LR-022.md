## Complete Implementation

Create three bounded challenges using the range: accuracy on fixed rings, timing on predictable moving targets and a short distance sequence. Retain real projectile hits. Give opponents brief distinct banter, an invitation, win/loss/decline and rematch. Offer a one-time modest supply reward per completed tier through existing transaction APIs; later attempts grant only score/banter. Show rules and score in ordinary activity UI without pausing world safety incorrectly.

## Persistence And Interruptions

Save tier completion, earned rewards and best scores independently. Cancel never awards victory; reload cannot replay rewards. Do not consume campaign arrows.
