Milestone: D

## Player Outcome

Give players a free, useful place to learn the existing bow.

## Affected Ownership

scripts/story_activity_director.gd; scripts/minigame_manager.gd or existing activity owner; existing bow/projectile/target code; Greyfen scene/staging; HUD

## Complete Implementation

Place a small range on an existing clear safe strip, preserving routes and NPCs. Use real bow aim/draw/projectile collision against physical targets. Offer timed and untimed practice, score based on hit location and clear cancel/retry. Training arrows belong to the activity and cannot enter InventoryManager, be recovered as real ammo or consume owned ammo. Restore pre-activity weapon, controls and camera on exit.

## Persistence And Interruptions

Persist only personal best if useful; no economy reward in practice. A save/load or interruption cannot retain training ammunition outside the range.

## Exclusions

No replacement bow physics, auto-hit targets, artificial ammo grants or mandatory tutorial.

## User Manual Acceptance

1. Practice with zero and full real ammunition and inspect counts after exit.
2. Aim/fire/cancel with mouse and controller.
3. Leave by cancellation/interruption and check camera/control restoration.
