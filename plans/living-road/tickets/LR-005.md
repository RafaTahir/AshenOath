## Complete Implementation

Organize retained items into equipment, supplies, ingredients and quest evidence without duplicating ownership. Show equipped blade/bow, plain effect descriptions, stack quantities, comparison for applicable gear and current quick-slot assignment. Provide name/type sorting, stable selection after purchase/use, empty states, scroll bounds and controller focus. Preserve actual existing quick actions and ingredient/evidence semantics. Do not turn non-owned journal evidence into tradable inventory.

## Persistence And Interruptions

Persist sorting/quick slots through existing saved settings/inventory; old saves retain valid assignments. Equipment comparison must not create separate live stat state.

## Exclusions

No carry-weight tax, durability grind, junk flood, loot generation or existing item deletion.

## User Manual Acceptance

1. Open every category, sort and inspect long descriptions at 720p.
2. Assign/use supplies and purchase items, confirming quantities and stable focus.
3. Save/Continue and operate the inventory without a mouse.
