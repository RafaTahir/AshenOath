## Complete Implementation

Make commitment explicit and exclusive unless a future separately authorized design changes it. A second commitment attempt surfaces the existing promise before acceptance; allow an honest conversation and ended relationship rather than silent overwrite. Author restrained reactions for affected people, with no humiliating trophy scene. Separate friendship support from romantic availability. Preserve private information boundaries and avoid witnesses commenting on events they could not know.

## Persistence And Interruptions

One commitment authority plus historical events; migrations never invent a partner. Keep ending facts deterministic and one-time scenes idempotent.

## Exclusions

No jealousy minigame, public punishment scene, hidden affection meter or compulsory romance.

2. End or decline a relationship and observe later dialogue.
3. Reload and confirm no double commitment or lost friendship/services.
