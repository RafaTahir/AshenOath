## Physical Work And Ownership

- Each quest keeps one work assembly, projected from its actual saved state;
  obsolete action points no longer leave duplicate assemblies at every stage.
- Ordinary work starts with Use, sheaths equipment and performs a short action.
  Movement, combat, hurt, pause, focus loss, dialogue and zone retirement release
  its pose. Dialogue and combat keep their existing higher priority.
- Door, bell, screen, snare, nursery, signal, toll and cover states follow actual
  objective/part progress. These props do not introduce solid route blockers.
- Carried supplies attach to the retained hand bone and disappear during weapon
  use; they are freed with the regional owner. No floating hand fallback replaces
  missing bones and no player inventory is consumed to represent supplied work.
- Bracken may notice nearby active track/scar points through the existing scent
  owner. The reaction never collects evidence or advances an objective.
- Scene performance uses nearby present actors; absent workers and departed
  people remain attributed records. A narrator presents recorded consequences,
  rather than making a live person recite third-person aftermath prose.

## Compatibility

- Quest, objective, choice, actor, encounter, reward and XP claim IDs remain.
  QuestManager owns progress; StoryState owns durable choices/parts; existing
  inventory/progression services own deliveries and once-only rewards.
- Part-site changes preserve inspected flags and completed parts. Independent
  clues remain usable in either order; full conclusions follow comparison.
- Bundled deliveries apply to new pickups for four repeated same-destination
  tasks. An old odd-part save still drops its already-held single piece normally,
  then collects the remaining supplied materials. No completed delivery is reset.
- Significant new help memories are written only on an actual newly completed
  task. Old saves do not receive fabricated relationship events or duplicate XP.
- Existing completed shrine/encounter migration, peaceful rewards, fitting effects,
  30 levels, 17 Marks, 20 practices, ending requirements and homecoming remain.
- Optional work, leisure victories, preparation and Bracken never gate an ending.
