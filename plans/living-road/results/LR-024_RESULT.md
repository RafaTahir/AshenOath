# LR-024 - A Greyfen Gathering

Implemented locally; UNVERIFIED - REQUIRES USER TESTING. No runtime checks or
publication. The existing common table gains a post-report invitation and linen
dressing. Attend, decline, leave and rejoin do not alter quests, games or prizes.
Three Marks, Draughts and the southern range retain their own normal interactions.

Rook, Tor, Anwen, Mira and Bracken offer short optional moments only when actually
present and approached. Report disclosure, the repaired stool and a real Mira
commitment inform specific lines. No forced romance, duplicate NPCs or vendor
relocation. Existing village ambience is retained rather than layering another loop.

Saved invitation, bounded two-game-hour window and completion are independent of
activity rewards. Day/time, danger and major story changes end staging. Sector
retirement removes attached dressing. Rejoining never resets prize or romance flags.

Files: `greyfen_gathering.gd`, `story_activity_director.gd`, `game.gd`.
No new binary assets; combined package follows LR-028. Static source review only.

Manual checklist: attend after the first report; use all three games and vendors;
talk to available friends/Bracken; decline or leave; save/reload and advance time;
confirm main progression and existing rewards stay unchanged.
